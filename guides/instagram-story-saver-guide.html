Instagram Stories: How Saving Works and What You Can Keep

Save Instagram Stories before they disappear, using Highlights, archives, and the official options.

Instagram Stories vanish after 24 hours, which is the whole point of the format, and also the reason people want to save them. The good news is that Instagram gives creators good tools for keeping their own Stories, and viewers have a few honest options too. This guide explains Highlights, the Archive, and what you can and cannot save from other people's Stories, so you do not fall for the apps that promise to save everything.

Highlights keep Stories alive

A creator can add a Story to a Highlight, which appears as a circle on their profile and stays there until they remove it. Highlights are the legitimate way to keep a Story visible, and they are how businesses keep menus, prices, and FAQs available. If you run a business account, organise Highlights by topic and give each one a clear cover image and name. Viewers can watch a Highlight at any time, and it does not disappear after 24 hours.

Your own Story Archive

Every Story you post is automatically saved to your Archive, which only you can see. Open your profile, tap the menu, then Archive, to find old Stories. From there you can re-share a Story, add it to a Highlight, or download it to your phone. This is the best way to keep your own Stories, because the original file is yours and Instagram keeps it for you. Check the Archive before you delete anything from your camera roll.

Saving someone else's Story

Instagram does not provide a download button for other people's Stories, and that is intentional: Stories are meant to be temporary and personal. The built-in options are a screenshot or a screen recording, which are fine for personal use, such as remembering an event detail, but not for reposting someone else's Story as your own. If a Story contains information you need, such as a discount code or an address, a screenshot is the practical answer.

Beware story-saver apps

Apps and websites that promise to download anyone's Stories usually ask for your Instagram login or show you a fake preview. Many are designed to steal accounts, and some display Stories from public accounts without permission, which raises privacy concerns. Instagram also detects and blocks many of these services, so they stop working without warning. If a Story is public and you want to keep it, a screenshot is safer and more honest than handing your password to a stranger's app.

Highlights for businesses

For a business, Highlights are the most valuable free space on Instagram, because they sit at the top of the profile and answer the questions every new visitor asks. The standard set is simple: a Highlight for prices or a menu, one for how to order or book, one for customer reviews, one for frequently asked questions, and one for behind-the-scenes content. Each Highlight should have a clear cover image and a short name, and the Stories inside it should be current: remove anything out of date, such as last year's prices or a finished promotion. Update Highlights monthly, and treat them as a living page rather than a one-time setup. A visitor who finds their answer in a Highlight is far more likely to become a customer than one who has to send a message and wait for a reply.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Stories last?

Stories disappear after 24 hours unless the creator adds them to a Highlight on their profile.

Can I save someone else's Story?

Instagram does not offer a download button for other people's Stories. Screenshots and screen recordings are the only built-in options, and you should respect the creator's privacy.

Where are my own Stories saved?

Your own Stories are kept in your Archive, and you can add them to Highlights that stay on your profile.
