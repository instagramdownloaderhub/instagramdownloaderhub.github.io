Instagram Video Formats: Reels, Feed Video, Stories, and Live

Every Instagram video format explained, with lengths, sizes, and how saving works for each one.

Instagram has four video formats, and they differ in length, audience, lifespan, and what you can save. Confusing them is easy, because a video can appear in more than one place, but each format has its own rules. This guide explains Reels, feed videos, Stories, and Live, so you know what you are looking at and what you can do with it.

Reels

Reels are short vertical videos, usually 15 to 90 seconds, built for discovery. They appear in a dedicated feed, they are recommended to people who do not follow you, and they can be downloaded when the creator allows it. Reels support music, effects, and text, and they are the format Instagram promotes most. If you want reach, make a Reel. If you want to keep one, the Save feature and the Share menu download option are the tools to use.

Feed videos

Feed videos are longer videos posted to the main feed, and they stay on the creator's profile grid. They can be several minutes long, they support long captions, and they are good for tutorials, talks, and detailed content. They can be saved with the bookmark icon, but Instagram does not offer a file download for them. Feed videos are watched by followers first, and they are less likely to be recommended to strangers than Reels.

Stories

Stories are vertical photos or videos that disappear after 24 hours. They are shown to followers at the top of the app, and they support polls, questions, and links. Stories are personal and temporary by design, and Instagram does not offer a download button for other people's Stories. Creators can save their own Stories to Highlights, which stay on their profile, or to their Archive.

Live video

Live is a real-time broadcast. Viewers can comment and react while it happens, and the creator can save the broadcast to their profile or camera roll when it ends. Live videos cannot be downloaded while they are live, and if the creator does not save the broadcast, it is gone. If you want to keep a Live you hosted, save it immediately after it ends, before you close the app.

Pick the right format for your goal

Each Instagram format serves a different goal, and choosing the wrong one wastes your effort. If your goal is to reach new people, make a Reel, because Reels are recommended to strangers. If your goal is to teach something in detail, make a carousel, because people swipe through steps and save it for later. If your goal is to stay in touch with followers, use Stories, because they appear at the top of the app and invite replies. If your goal is to talk to your audience live, use Live, because real-time conversation builds a stronger connection than any recorded video. Many creators use all four in a week: a Reel for discovery, a carousel for depth, Stories for daily contact, and a Live session once a month. The format is a tool, and the right tool depends on the job you want it to do.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long can a Reel be?

Reels can be up to 90 seconds when recorded in the app, and up to 15 minutes when uploaded as a video file in some cases.

What is the difference between a Story and a Reel?

A Story disappears after 24 hours and is shown to followers. A Reel is permanent, vertical, and recommended to new people.

Can I save a Live video?

A Live video can be saved to IGTV or your camera roll after it ends, if the creator chooses to save it.
