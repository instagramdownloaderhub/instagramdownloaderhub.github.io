Instagram Profile Pic Viewer

View a public Instagram profile picture at a larger size. Works for public accounts; private accounts are protected.

Limits, stated plainly: Instagram does not offer a public profile-picture API. This tool tries a public mirror service to display the picture for public accounts, and always links to the real profile. For private accounts only the small thumbnail is visible, and no tool can fetch the larger image. Never reuse someone's profile picture without permission.
